## About the Holy Name Society

The Holy Name Society is a Catholic men's organization dedicated to
promoting devotion to the Holy Name of Jesus, supporting the parish
community, and encouraging fellowship and service among its members.

For more information about the society, please visit the
[About page]({{ '/about/' | relative_url }}).
